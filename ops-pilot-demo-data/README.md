# OpsPilot AI Synthetic Data (v2.3)

## Architecture
Fictional retail architecture consisting of 9 core services (Frontend, OMS, WMS, Payment, Inventory, Redis, PG-OMS, PG-WMS, Kafka).

## Dataset Contents
* `topology/`: Service definitions and dependency mappings.
* `incidents/`: 40 historical incidents and 12 demo scenarios.
* `deployments/`: 30 mock CI/CD deployments.
* `telemetry/`: Time-series snapshots mapping directly to incidents.
* `runbooks/`: 13 service-specific operational guides.

All data is strictly synthetic and intentionally designed for testing Phase 1 of OpsPilot AI.
